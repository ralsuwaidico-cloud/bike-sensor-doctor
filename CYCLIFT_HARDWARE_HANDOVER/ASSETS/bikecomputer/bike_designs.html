Bike Computer · interface exploration

Six ways to ride the same data

Six distinct cycling-computer concepts for the Nokia 5.3 (and, later, the round Waveshare display). Every concept shows live power, cadence and heart rate, five progressive HR zones, a circular coaching indicator with a target marker, plain-language coaching, ride time, distance, a performance graph and Bluetooth status. Numbers are demonstration data, not a live ride. Tap a card to jump to its full preview; each concept also shows the Z1→Z5 gauge progression and the three coaching states.
