## What Cyclift is
An iPhone app (plan generation and editing with preview → apply, rule‑based coach, ride recording with GPS, strength
sessions, Progress, Recovery from Apple Health, Apple Watch companion) that is the **brain and recorder**. Devices
extend it: the iPhone owns the plan, FTP, GPS, workout identity and Health writes; devices show live data, run the
ride clock when they are the recorder (BikeComputer), and send back events.

## Device A — BikeComputer (Waveshare ESP32‑S3‑Touch‑LCD‑1.28‑B)
- Handlebar computer, 1.28" round 240×240 touch, no keys, USB‑C for programming via the owner's Windows PC kits.
- Purpose: live power/cadence from his XDS power meter, HR strap, cadence/speed sensor; three approved ride dashboards
  (Power Focus default / Split / Coach First), pull‑down controls (hold PAUSE/END), results/history, Wi‑Fi, power
  states, diagnostics, and a BLE "Link" to the iPhone (protocol 1.0 → 1.2.3 shipped; 1.3 phone‑first implemented).
- Firmware: ESP‑IDF 5.5.1 + LVGL 8, branch `fw/phonefirst` @ c0a10fb0, version **0.7.4‑rc5** (host/simulator/BLE
  harness green, five QA rounds, kit built) — **not installed**. The board currently runs **0.7.3**, which crashes at

- Hardware verified only up to 0.6.0 rev 1 usage and the 0.7.3 crash logs. The project's first milestone — real power

## How the devices will integrate with the iPhone
Adapters on the phone (`DeviceLink`, `CyWatchLink`, `WatchLink`, `BLESensors`) produce values with source and age;
`SensorHub` / `RideRecorder` / `Store` decide. Features are gated on capability bits (BikeComputer INFO caps) or the
frame version (watch `ver`); unknown TLV tags are skipped; events carry (deviceId, seq) and are acknowledged
cumulatively; one recorder per ride; the phone writes Health once per workout. See 05_INTEGRATION_CONTRACTS.md.
