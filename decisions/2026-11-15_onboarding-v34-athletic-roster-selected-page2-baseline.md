# Decision — Onboarding v3.4 Athletic Roster selected as the Page 2 design baseline

**Date:** 15 Nov 2026 · **Decided by:** owner · **Status of the design:** **Selected Page 2 Design Baseline — not implemented, not tested.**
Reference: `product/onboarding/v34/ONBOARDING_V34_PAGE2_ATHLETIC_ROSTER.md` v0.1 (commit `b521cbf3`), six sheets.

1. The Athletic Roster is the selected Page 2 visual direction: main sport or sports; Endurance-led / Balanced / Strength-led when both disciplines are present; additional training activities; one activity-library entry; recovery interests separate from training; explicit planning and recording capability; compact presentation for many sports.
2. **Page 3 — Goals:** the priority selector is removed. Page 2's training priority is the single authoritative setting. Goal selection and the main goal stay independent of it.
3. **Page 7 — Athletic Blueprint:** the sporting identity is one readable line, for example "Road cycling + Gym training · Balanced". The decorative icon collection is not reintroduced.
4. Before implementation, the capability classification of indoor cycling, mountain biking and bodyweight training must be validated against the app. Reusing a recording engine is not full support for independent planning, coaching or sport-specific metrics. (Validation result: `release/consolidation/D_ARCHITECTURE_AND_DATA_INTEGRATION.md` §3.)
5. No unsupported activity enters the planner without an approved capability contract.
6. Earlier onboarding documents are preserved as historical references.

Not decided here: visual approval of onboarding pages 1 and 3–7 beyond the two changes above (the v3.3 seven-page structure is the current direction); any implementation.
