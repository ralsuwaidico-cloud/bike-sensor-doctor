# Decision — Global navigation: three tabs, Train on the right, Profile from the Home avatar (owner decision D)

Date: 14 Nov 2026 · Decided by: owner · Recorded by: Claude · Level 1 (decision record)

## Decision (design direction only)
1. Navigation is Home, Plan, Progress as ordinary tabs, and Train as a distinct, larger control at the far right.
2. The dedicated Profile tab is removed from the proposed design. The Home avatar is the entry to the complete Profile.
3. Profile sections: Athlete · Performance Setup · Training Preferences · Devices & Connections · Settings & Support. One owner per setting.

## Boundary
Authorises design review and static mock-ups only. **No** code, navigation change, onboarding change, data-model change,
merge, change to Home, Plan, Progress or Train, firmware change or build.
This is not permission to modify the existing production navigation.

## Unchanged
Training Hub v1.4 remains the approved design baseline. Its six pending matters and the two open test defects remain pending.
No Train symbol is approved (Shift, Step, Frame, Pulse and all earlier candidates included).
