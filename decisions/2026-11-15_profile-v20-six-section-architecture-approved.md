# Decision — Profile v2.0: six-section information architecture approved; quick menu retired in the design

**Date:** 15 Nov 2026 · **Decided by:** owner (brief "Profile v2.0 — Information Architecture & Premium Visual Design") · **Recorded by:** Claude

1. Profile has **exactly six sections**: Athlete Profile · Training Setup · Performance Settings · Devices & Connections · App Preferences · Data, Help & About. Status: **Approved information architecture.** This replaces the five-section list in `product/profile/PROFILE_AND_NAVIGATION_V1_DESIGN.md` and the seven-section Phase 0 proposal; both documents are preserved unedited.
2. Profile is reached **only** from the Home avatar. The five-option Home quick menu is replaced, **in the design**, by direct navigation to the Profile landing page. This is an approved design direction — **not** permission to change the app (settles conflict register item 51 as a design direction).
3. Ownership: Profile — who the athlete is and how Cyclift is configured; Plan — goals, schedules, programmed workouts, routines; Train — starting and recording; Progress — development and history; Home — overview. Profile links, never duplicates.
4. Visual design of Profile v2.0: **Pending Review.** Implementation: **Not Authorised.**
