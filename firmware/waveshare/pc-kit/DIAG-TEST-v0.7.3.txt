BikeComputer 0.7.3 + Cyclift - diagnostic report test (one session, about 15 minutes, no crash needed)
=====================================================================================================
You need: the board with 0.7.3, the power meter on the bike, your Apple Watch, the iPhone with the Cyclift build that has
"BikeComputer diagnostic reports" (Claude tells you the build number). Do NOT start a ride on the board during steps 2-7.
Write PASS or FAIL after each step.

1  Cyclift open, board connected (board: Settings > Phone shows "connected"). Pedal: Cyclift shows watts and cadence
   from the power meter and heart rate from the watch.                                                      PASS/FAIL
2  On the board: Settings > Diagnostics, scroll down, press and HOLD "HOLD: SAVE REPORT" for 1 second.
   The board shows "Report N saved". Do it 3 times (3 reports), a few seconds apart.                       PASS/FAIL
3  In Cyclift: Connections > BikeComputer. Within a few seconds, without pressing anything, it shows
   "Diagnostic report ready" with a progress bar ("... of ... KB").                                         PASS/FAIL
4  When the first report shows "Report saved" and the bar of the next one is moving: iPhone Settings > Bluetooth OFF.
   Note the "... of ... KB" number. Wait 10 seconds.                                                       DONE
5  Bluetooth ON again. Do not touch the board. Within about 30 seconds Cyclift reconnects by itself and the bar goes on
   from the number you noted (not from 0).                                                                 PASS/FAIL
6  All three arrive: "Report saved". Tap "BikeComputer reports": open the newest. The text starts with [header], shows
   "kind 3 manual", and the line under it says "checksum ........ verified" and "confirmed by the board".   PASS/FAIL
7  Tap Share and save it to Files (or mail it to yourself). The .txt file opens.                          PASS/FAIL
8  During steps 3-6 keep pedalling now and then: watts, cadence and heart rate kept updating every second in Cyclift
   and on the board; no "stale", no restart of the board.                                                   PASS/FAIL
9  (optional) Hold SAVE REPORT once more, then START a ride on the board: the report waits ("Diagnostic report ready",
   bar not moving) while the ride records; END the ride: it arrives within about 20 seconds.               PASS/FAIL/SKIPPED

If anything fails: run CAPTURE-CRASH.bat only if the board restarted; otherwise just tell Claude the step number.
