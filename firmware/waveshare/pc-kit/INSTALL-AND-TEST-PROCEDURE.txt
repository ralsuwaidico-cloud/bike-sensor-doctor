BikeComputer - ONE combined update: firmware 0.7.0 + iPhone app 0.8.0 (build 40)         26 Oct 2026
=====================================================================================================
Order matters: board first, then the phone app. Both speak Link protocol 1.2.1 and the same profile schema.
Board on USB with the battery DISCONNECTED (HW-1: USB charging with the 500 mAh cell is NOT verified safe).

A. Board - firmware 0.7.0  (needs your approval; nothing is flashed until you run the installer)
 1. Connect the board by USB. Double-click INSTALL-v0.7.0.bat.
 2. It reads a full 16 MB backup (about 3.5 min) and must print "Backup verified: 16 MB, firmware <what is on the board>".
    It accepts 0.6.1, 0.6.0 rev 1/2/3, 0.5.0 or 0.4.0. If it STOPS, nothing was changed - send me the message.
 3. Type YES. It writes 2 regions only (app + OTA selector). Settings, FTP, pairings and rides are kept.
 4. The board restarts to Home. Settings footer must read "firmware 0.7.0".
    Rollback at any time (data kept): ROLLBACK-TO-v061.bat (touch fix, old pairing) or ROLLBACK-TO-v050.bat.
    Exact image incl. data as it was before step 2: RESTORE-BACKUP-BEFORE-v070.bat.
    Touch ever dead: press RESET. SAFE MODE: hold BOOT while pressing RESET (touch gate off for that boot).

B. iPhone - TestFlight build 40 (0.8.0)
 5. TestFlight > BikeComputer > install build 40. Your data (profile, rides, templates, plan) is kept.
    Previous build 36 stays available under "Previous builds".

C. Pair the two (first time after the board's data reset)
 6. On the board: Connections > Phone. If it says "REPAIR NEEDED", hold REPAIR (the board takes a new Bluetooth
    identity). Otherwise tap CONNECT.
 7. iPhone: if iOS Settings > Bluetooth still lists "BikeComputer Link", tap (i) > Forget This Device (only step
    the app cannot do for you).
 8. App > Connections > BikeComputer > Connect. Stages must advance Searching > Found > Pairing (type the 6-digit
    code shown on the board) > Authenticating > Connected > Syncing profile > Ready.
 9. The profile-differences dialog explains that the phone profile will be applied - confirm. Board Settings > Rider
    must now show your phone's name/FTP/weight; app shows "synced hh:mm".

D. Test - record PASS / FAIL / NOT TESTED, with notes
 10. TEST-v0.7.0.txt  (board + both devices: 10 pairing points, PHONE screen, touch/wake 20 cycles, Connections, Wi-Fi)
 11. BUILD40-TEST.txt (app: pairing stages, Repair, profile sync + validation, routines journey, Train now, both/rest
     days, offline, restart, migration)
Everything in both releases is simulator/automated-tested only until you confirm it here.
