PHONE LINK TEST - firmware 0.4.0            (print or keep on the phone)
=====================================================================
What 0.4.0 adds: the Waveshare can be paired with the iPhone app (build 11+).
The phone sends GPS speed, the time and the Apple Watch heart rate; the
Waveshare records distance itself and keeps riding if the phone disappears.
Everything from 0.2.1 / 0.3.0 is unchanged (onboarding, ride screens A/B/C,
coach, history, settings, Wi-Fi). Your settings and rides are kept.
