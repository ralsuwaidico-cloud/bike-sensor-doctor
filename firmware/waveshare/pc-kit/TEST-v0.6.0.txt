BikeComputer firmware 0.6.0 (rev 3) — hardware test checklist (25 Oct 2026)
==============================================================================
Record PASS / FAIL / NOT TESTED per step, with numbers where asked. Everything in 0.6.0 is SIMULATOR-TESTED ONLY
until you confirm it here; the simulator cannot see the real touch controller, radio or panel. Board on USB with the
battery DISCONNECTED (HW-1), except the optional battery step G2 (battery alone, never USB and battery together).
iPhone app: build 24 or newer (profile / settings / Wi-Fi from the phone need the app 0.4.0+ = build 24+).
