BikeComputer firmware 0.6.0 — hardware test checklist (25 Oct 2026)
======================================================================
Record PASS / FAIL / NOT TESTED per step, with numbers where asked. Everything in 0.6.0 is simulator-tested only
until you confirm it here. Board on USB with the battery DISCONNECTED (HW-1), except step P8 which needs the battery
alone (never USB and battery together). iPhone app: build 21 or newer (build 21 cannot yet use Wi-Fi setup / profile
sync from the phone; those arrive with the next build — steps marked [app 0.4.0+] wait for it).

Install
 I1  INSTALL-v0.6.0.bat: finds COM port, reads the 16 MB backup, says "Backup verified: 16 MB, firmware 0.5.0",
     asks YES, writes 2 regions, "INSTALLED". Board restarts to Home with your name and FTP.
 I2  Settings > Diagnostics > Device Health: Firmware 0.6.0. History still lists your rides; power meter still paired.
     (Rollback at any time: ROLLBACK-TO-v050.bat keeps data; RESTORE-BACKUP-BEFORE-v060.bat = exact image.)
