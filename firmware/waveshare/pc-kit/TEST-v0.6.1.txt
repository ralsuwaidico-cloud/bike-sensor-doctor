BikeComputer firmware 0.6.1 — hardware acceptance checklist (25 Oct 2026)
=========================================================================
0.6.1 = 0.6.0 (rev 3) + the fix for "touch dead after screen-off / sleep wake" + Settings layout fixes. Nothing else changed.
Record PASS / FAIL / NOT TESTED per step. Everything below is SIMULATOR-TESTED ONLY until you confirm it here; the simulator
now emulates the real touch controller's event-driven reads and reproduced the 0.6.0 freeze (20/20 cycles dead) before the
fix and 0/20 after it — but only the glass can prove it. Board on USB, battery DISCONNECTED (HW-1).

Install
 I1  INSTALL-v0.6.1.bat: finds the COM port, reads the 16 MB backup, says "Backup verified: 16 MB, firmware 0.6.0 rev 3",
     asks YES, writes 2 regions, "INSTALLED". Board restarts to Home with your name and FTP.   PASS/FAIL
 I2  Settings footer: "firmware 0.6.1" and a build stamp "Oct 25 hh:mm id xxxxxxxx". History lists your rides; the power
     meter is still paired.   PASS/FAIL
     Rollback at any time: ROLLBACK-TO-v050.bat (0.5.0, keeps data) or RESTORE-BACKUP-BEFORE-v061.bat (exact image).
     If touch is ever dead: press RESET (always works). SAFE MODE: hold BOOT while pressing RESET -> red "SAFE MODE" badge
     on Home, the touch gate is off for that boot, nothing saved or erased.

Serial log (optional but very useful): READ-LOG-v0.6.0.bat works for 0.6.1 too. Lines starting with "TOUCH" show the
