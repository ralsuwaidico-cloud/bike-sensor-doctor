# Hardware risk register — BikeComputer (Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.28-B)

| # | Risk | Status | Detail | Mitigation / decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HW-1 | **USB-C charging current exceeds the battery rating** | OPEN — charging with this battery is **not verified safe** | Charger ETA6096 with R15 = 160 kΩ (schematic Rev3) sets ≈1 A fast charge. Fitted cell: HXJNLDC 503030, 500 mAh, max charge 0.5 A (seller spec). 1 A ≈ 2 C. Firmware cannot change the current (no STAT/ISET control). Pre-charge 200 mA and termination 130 mA are fixed in the chip. | Guide prepared for replacing R15 with 470 kΩ (predicted 340–380 mA), including before/after battery-lead measurements (not USB current). Rashed decided on 24 Oct 2026 to defer the hardware change; the battery remains connected and powers the device. Firmware never shows "charging" as a confirmed state and labels the percentage as an estimate. Re-open when the modification is scheduled. |
| HW-2 | No charge-status or VBUS sense to the ESP32 | ACCEPTED | STAT pin not routed; no VBUS net to a GPIO. | Firmware infers "USB power likely" from voltage only and says so. |
| HW-3 | No true power-off | ACCEPTED | No power switch, charger/regulator always on. | Deep sleep is the lowest state; UI labels it "Deep sleep (lowest power)" with recovery instructions. |
| HW-4 | Deep-sleep current unknown | OPEN | Board figure unpublished. | Measure by voltage slope once 0.5.0 is on hardware. |
| HW-5 | Touch wake unreliable (reported on 0.5.0: many taps needed) | OPEN — reworked in 0.6.0, simulator-tested only | Screen-off: the CST816S auto-slept 2 s after the last touch and its first touch only woke the controller (lost tap) → 0.6.0 keeps it awake while the screen is off (DisAutoSleep 0xFE, ~1.5 mA) and widens the detector (2.5 s window, 20–400 ms presses, any position). Deep sleep: the waking touch counts as tap 1, 2.5 s for the rest, setting kept in RTC memory. | RESET and USB plug remain the dependable wake and the Deep-sleep page says so. Verify with TEST-v0.6.0.txt C1–C5 (screen off) and D1–D4 (deep sleep): ≥ 19/20 and 0 false wakes before relying on it. HW-4 (sleep current) in the same session. |
| HW-6 | Phone link lost when the screen went off (0.5.0) | FIXED in 0.6.0 (software), not yet hardware-verified | Not hardware: (a) advertising was never switched on at boot, only by the Settings toggle, so every restart / deep-sleep wake left the board invisible to the phone; (b) auto deep sleep defaulted to 10 min and misjudged USB power with the battery unplugged. | 0.6.0 enables the link at boot from the profile, defaults auto deep sleep to off (migrating 0.5.0 profiles), never sleeps with a ride or a connected phone, and adds connected standby. Verify with TEST-v0.6.0.txt A1–A5, D4. |
| HW-7 | Charging notifications requested (80/90/98 %, beeps) | NOT FEASIBLE on this board — alerts disabled | No charge/USB sense line, no buzzer or speaker, percentage is a voltage estimate (no fuel gauge), and HW-1 is open. | `CHARGE_ALERTS.md`: Settings › Power says "Charging alerts: unavailable"; a phone EVENT (estimate-labelled) is prepared behind a default-off flag for after HW-1 is resolved. No continuous alarm, no forced brightness, no claim of replacing the charger's protection. |
