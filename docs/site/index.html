Cyclift

Cycling and strength training in one weekly plan, with one coach. Record rides with your phone's GPS, standard Bluetooth sensors or Apple Watch, log strength sessions set by set, and get one clear, explained suggestion for your next workout — from your own data.

Works offline, no account required. An optional account (Sign in with Apple, Google or e-mail) syncs your training between devices. No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

Works with what you have

The phone alone records rides with GPS. Add any of these if you own them: Bluetooth heart-rate monitors, power meters, cadence and speed sensors that use the standard Bluetooth fitness profiles; Apple Watch for heart rate; Apple Health for importing and saving workouts. Each live value shows which source it came from. No ride needs a power meter.

Optional BikeComputer device

Cyclift can also pair with the BikeComputer, an optional handlebar device with its own display that relays your power meter and sensors. It is one supported accessory among others; every training feature works without it.

Training suggestions are rule-based estimates from what you record. Cyclift is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.
