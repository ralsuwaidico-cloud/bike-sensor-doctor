Support

Questions, problems or feedback: e-mail SUPPORT_EMAIL. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the Athletics version (Profile › About).

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Guest mode keeps everything on your phone and works offline. An account only adds sync between devices.

How do I delete my account and data? In the app: Profile › Account › Delete account and all data. This removes your account and every record from our servers and from the phone immediately.

How do I export my data? Profile › Account › Export my data creates a complete JSON file.

Do I need the BikeComputer device? No. Rides can be recorded with the phone's GPS alone; the device adds power-meter data and a handlebar display.

The BikeComputer will not connect after I reset it. Open Profile › Connections & devices › BikeComputer and follow Repair. If iOS Settings › Bluetooth still lists "BikeComputer Link", tap it and choose Forget This Device first.

Apple Health. Permissions can be changed any time in the iOS Settings app › Health › Data Access & Devices › Athletics.
