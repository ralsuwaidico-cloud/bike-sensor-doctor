Support

Questions, problems or feedback: e-mail ralsuwaidico@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the Cyclift version (Profile › About).

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Cyclift 1.0 has no accounts and no sign-in. Everything stays on your iPhone and the app works offline.

Which sensors work? Standard Bluetooth heart-rate monitors, power meters, and speed / cadence sensors (the standard Bluetooth fitness profiles) pair directly with the iPhone in Profile › Connections & devices. A watch or band that broadcasts heart rate over standard Bluetooth can be added as a heart-rate sensor while it is broadcasting.

Apple Watch. Install Cyclift on the watch (Watch app › Available Apps), open it and tap Start cycling: heart rate is sent to the iPhone during the ride, and at the end you choose whether the workout is saved to Apple Health.

Do I need a power meter or the BikeComputer device? No. Rides can be recorded with the iPhone's GPS alone, with heart rate, speed, cadence or perceived effort guiding the coach. The BikeComputer is an optional handlebar device; every training feature works without it.

The BikeComputer will not connect after I reset it. Open Profile › Connections & devices › BikeComputer and follow Repair. If iOS Settings › Bluetooth still lists "BikeComputer Link", tap it and choose Forget This Device first.

How do I export my data? Profile › Your data › Export my data creates a complete JSON file you can save or share.

How do I delete my data? Profile › Your data › Delete all data on this phone, then confirm. This permanently removes your profile, plan, rides, strength sessions, templates and remembered devices from the iPhone. Deleting the app also removes all its data. There is no copy anywhere else: the developer never receives your data.

Apple Health. Permissions can be changed any time in the iOS Settings app › Health › Data Access & Devices › Cyclift.

Location. Cyclift asks for location "While Using the App" and uses it only while you record a ride (also with the screen locked).
