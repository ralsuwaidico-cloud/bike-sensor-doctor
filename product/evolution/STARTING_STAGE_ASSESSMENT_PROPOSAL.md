# Athlete Evolution — proposed starting-stage assessment method

**Status: PROPOSAL FOR THE OWNER'S DECISION. Nothing in this document is enabled in the app.**
Prepared 22 Nov 2026 in response to the Screen 7 design direction (22 Nov 2026, 17:33).
Separate from the Screen 7 interface, which shows "Starting stage not yet assessed" for every athlete until a method is approved.

---

## 1. What this proposal is, and is not

It is a **method**: which information is used, in what order, how sports are combined, and how the result is labelled and explained.

It is **not** a set of approved thresholds. Wherever a number or a cut-off is needed, this document either leaves it blank ("to be set") or marks it clearly as a **placeholder to react to**. No placeholder has a scientific source behind it; each is a product judgement for you to accept, change or send for expert review.

## 2. The two kinds of stage

| | Estimated stage | Verified stage |
|---|---|---|
| Comes from | Onboarding answers | Approved criteria met by recorded training |
| Label on screen | "Estimated · based on your answers" | "Verified · from your recorded training" |
| Can be given on day one | Yes | No |
| Highest stage it can give | **Capped** (see principle 2) | G5 |

A third state, **Exploring**, is only what the athlete is looking at on the rail. It is never stored and plays no part in this method.

## 3. Principles

1. **An estimate is a starting guess, not an achievement.** It is always labelled as estimated and always explains itself in one sentence.
2. **Answers alone cannot reach the upper stages.** Proposed rule: an estimate can never be higher than a ceiling stage; everything above the ceiling must be verified by recorded training. *Placeholder ceiling: G2.* This is what guarantees that choosing "Intermediate" or "Advanced" can never produce G3 by itself.
3. **Sport by sport first, overall second.** Each sport on the athlete's roster gets its own reading; the overall stage is derived from those.
4. **Missing information is left missing.** A sport whose experience was never asked is "not assessed" — it neither raises nor lowers the estimate, and no measurement is assumed for it.
5. **When in doubt, the lower stage.** Over-placing an athlete risks unsuitable expectations; under-placing is corrected quickly by recorded training.
6. **Assessment never changes training by itself.** The stage is information. Any effect on plans would be a separate decision under the existing Coach approval rules.

## 4. What information exists today

| Information | Available today? | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Main and additional sports | Yes | Athletic Roster |
| Declared cycling experience | Yes | Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced |
| Declared strength experience | Yes | Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced |
| Declared experience in running, swimming, walking, mobility | **No** | Onboarding does not ask; would need a new question (a change to an approved screen) |
| Training history (how long, how often) | **No** | Not asked today; would need new questions |
| Chosen week (days, sessions, durations) | Yes | A stated intention, not history |
| Cycling threshold power (FTP) | Only if the athlete entered or tested it in Performance Settings | Entered and tested must stay distinguished |
| Recorded rides and strength sessions | Yes, once recorded | A new athlete has none |
| Imported history (Apple Health, Garmin, WHOOP) | **No** | Not available in Lab; integrations unverified |
| Recorded sessions for other sports | **No** | No recorder exists yet |

Consequence: with today's information, an estimate can rest only on roster plus declared cycling and strength experience. That is thin, which is why the ceiling in principle 2 matters.

## 5. The proposed method

**Step 1 — Read each sport.** Each sport with a declared experience level maps to a *range* of stages, not a single stage.

| Declared experience | Proposed range (placeholder) |
|---|---|
| Beginner | G0 – G1 |
| Intermediate | G1 – G2 |
| Advanced | G2 (the ceiling) |
| Not asked | Not assessed |

**Step 2 — Place within the range using training history.** Only if history is available (declared through new questions, or recorded). Without history, the estimate takes the **lower end** of the range (principle 5). The history questions and their cut-offs are to be set.

**Step 3 — Combine the sports into one overall estimate.** Three options; a decision is needed.

- **Option A — Main sport leads (recommended).** The main sport's range sets the overall range; the other assessed sports decide the position inside it (all at or above the main sport's level → upper end; any below → lower end).
- **Option B — Most cautious sport.** The overall estimate is the lowest of the assessed sports.
- **Option C — No overall estimate.** Show a stage per sport only.

Option A is recommended because the stage then reflects what the athlete mainly does, while a weaker second sport still holds it back, and each sport's own reading stays visible on the page.

**Step 4 — Verification (later, separate approval).** A verified stage needs approved criteria per stage. This proposal suggests only the *kinds* of evidence; the amounts are yours and, ideally, a qualified coach's to set.

| Kind of evidence | Example of what could be counted | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Consistency | Weeks with the planned sessions completed | to be set |
| Volume | Recorded training time over a period | to be set |
| Measured performance | A tested FTP; recorded strength lifts | to be set |
| Breadth | Evidence in each of the athlete's main sports | to be set |
| Durability | The above sustained over a long period | to be set |

Sports without a recorder cannot contribute verified evidence until recording exists for them.

**Step 5 — Show and explain.** The page shows the stage, its label, and one sentence saying why, e.g. "Estimated from: Cycling · Intermediate, Strength · Beginner."

## 6. Worked examples

These illustrate the method **using the placeholders above and Option A**. They are not approved outcomes.

| Athlete | Step 1 ranges | History | Estimate | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cycling (main) Intermediate + Strength Beginner | Cycling G1–G2; Strength G0–G1 | None | **G1, estimated** | Main sport gives G1–G2; a weaker second sport and no history → lower end |
| Same athlete, with declared regular training history | as above | Meets the (to be set) history condition | **G1 or G2 — depends on the decision in 7.4** | Whether history can outweigh a weaker second sport |
| Cycling (main) Advanced + Strength Advanced | Both G2 | None | **G2, estimated** | Ceiling; G3 and above need recorded evidence |
| Strength (main) Beginner only | Strength G0–G1 | None | **G0, estimated** | Lower end without history |
| Running (main) only | Not assessed | — | **Starting stage not yet assessed** | Running experience is not asked and running is not recorded |
| Running (main) + Cycling Intermediate | Running not assessed; Cycling G1–G2 | None | **Decision needed (7.5)** | Main sport has no reading |
| Any athlete, after months of recorded training | — | Recorded | **Verified stage, by approved criteria** | Replaces the estimate |

On your own example — intermediate cycling with beginner strength — the method gives an estimated G1 or G2 and can never give G3.

## 7. Decisions needed from you

1. **Ceiling for estimates.** G2 as proposed, or another stage?
2. **Step 1 ranges.** Accept, or change, the Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced ranges.
3. **Combining sports.** Option A, B or C.
4. **History against a weaker second sport.** Can good training history lift the estimate to the upper end even when a second sport is weaker?
5. **Main sport with no reading** (for example a runner who also cycles). Not assessed overall, or estimate from the sports that can be read, labelled accordingly?
6. **New onboarding questions.** Whether to add (a) experience for running, swimming and other sports and (b) training history. Both change approved screens, so they need your authorization.
7. **Does the chosen week count?** Proposed: no — it is an intention, not history.
8. **Does an entered (untested) FTP count?** Proposed: not toward the estimate; a tested FTP counts only toward verification.
9. **Verified criteria.** Who sets the thresholds, and whether to seek a qualified coach's review before approval.
10. **Can a stage go down?** For example, when a verified stage is lower than the estimate, or after a long break.
11. **Relationship to Progress.** Whether Progress shows the same estimated stage (it currently states that no stage is assigned).

## 8. What stays switched off until you approve

- Every athlete's status remains "Starting stage not yet assessed".
- No estimated or verified stage is calculated, stored, or shown anywhere.
- Progress, plans, prescriptions and athlete records are unchanged.
- The stage names (Origin, Foundation, Development, Performance, Refinement, Mastery) and their one-line descriptions remain provisional working labels.
