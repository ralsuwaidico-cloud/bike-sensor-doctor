Liquid Dock

Navigation

Try it
Tap the tabs in the dock below. The selection flows to the new tab with a short spring and a slight stretch, then settles. With Reduce Motion on (toolbar), it moves instantly.
Centre button state
The centre button always opens the Training Hub. Its look reflects today's state (from the existing homeToday()):
Pending: training icon, soft teal glow with a slow breathing pulse (3.2 s). The pulse stops after the hub has been opened once today.
Accessibility
VoiceOver: "Home, tab 1 of 5, selected" · centre: "Training hub, one workout pending"
Touch targets 64 pt tall, ≥ 72 pt wide; centre 54 pt
Dynamic Type: labels cap at XXL; icons stay
Safe area: dock sits above the home indicator
Haptic: one light selection tap on tab change; none while a ride is recording
During a recorded ride the dock is hidden; ride controls take the space
Compared with today
Today: fixed opaque bar, 58 pt raised centre, no motion, no haptics. Proposed: floating translucent capsule 14 pt from the edges, same five destinations in the same order.
