Production proposal

Simplest practical architecture

Offline-first on the Nokia, one small cloud service, and the ESP32 syncing through the phone. Nothing on this page changes the Android app yet.

1 · Device (Nokia app, later ESP32)

  • Local database (Room/SQLite) is the source of truth while riding. Rides are written as immutable records with a UUID, rider id and device id.
  • Rider switch loads profile, zones, coach config and sensor pairing from the local store; the active rider id is stamped on every ride record at start.
  • Works with no internet forever; sync is a background job.
  • ESP32-S3: records to flash, pairs with the phone over BLE; the phone app relays rides and receives profile/zone updates. Optional Wi-Fi sync later.

2 · Cloud (one managed backend, e.g. Supabase or Firebase)

  • Auth: e-mail/password and Google sign-in, tokens cached for offline use.
  • Tables: riders, profiles, rides, ride_samples (optional), clubs, memberships, invites, challenges. Row-level security: a rider reads only their own rows plus club members' shared fields.
  • Leaderboards computed server-side from verified rides only (device-recorded, sensor-sourced, not edited), per club, per period; a small scheduled job rolls weekly/monthly periods.
  • Health fields (HR, zones, FTP, body weight) are private by default; a per-field share flag exposes them to a club.

3 · Sync without conflicts

  • Rides: append-only, idempotent upload keyed by UUID — re-sending is harmless, two devices never edit the same ride.
  • Profile fields: last-writer-wins per field with a timestamp; verified values (tested LTHR, verified FTP) carry a "verified" flag and are never overwritten by estimates.
  • Feedback/answers: append-only log, folded into the profile on the device.
  • Club membership and invites: server-authoritative; the device caches a copy.

4 · Development stages

  1. Local accounts in the APK: rider list, switch before ride, per-rider prefs and ride files (no cloud). Acceptance: two riders, two rides, nothing mixed.
  2. Onboarding + personal coach ported from this prototype into the app; profile drives CoachEngine config.
  3. Cloud sign-in + sync of profiles and rides; offline queue; conflict rules above with tests.
  4. Clubs and leaderboard: invites, membership, server-computed boards, verified-rides-only rule.
  5. ESP32 relay: phone↔device BLE sync of rides and profiles.
  6. Verified challenges only after power verification and a fixed FTP-test protocol.
