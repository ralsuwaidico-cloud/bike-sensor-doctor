Comparison

Three rides side by side

Same route each time (your map shows the same out-and-back). Real columns come straight from Apple Fitness. Estimated columns are read from the shape of your heart-rate chart and would need the 1 Hz recording to be exact. Simulated columns depend on the power model and prove nothing about fitness.

Apple's zone bands (Z2 = 151–160 bpm) differ from the training model the coach uses here (Zone 2 = 138–149 bpm, estimated from a lactate-threshold heart rate of about 166, which is what you sustained for 30 minutes on 5 Oct). Neither is measured. The app's current default (max HR 185 → Zone 2 = 111–129 bpm) would have called all three rides "above target" from start to finish, which is why calibration is step one of the roadmap.
