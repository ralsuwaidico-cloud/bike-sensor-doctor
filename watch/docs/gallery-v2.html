2 · Before / after, with the decision behind each change

Cockpit

Before: two text rows of tiles, an interval line and a two-line coaching sentence competed with the number; two buttons took the bottom third. After: one 58 px number with a 20 px unit, a 270° orbital gauge (zone ladder + target band + white position marker), at most two secondary metrics, ONE command of ≤ 16 characters derived from the displayed target, and a single compact Pause pill; Lap and End moved to a controls overlay and the physical keys. The 214 W / 'reduce power' contradiction cannot recur: the command is computed from the same band shown above it (214 inside 180–216 → HOLD 198 W).

Performance

Before: a 46 px tall sparkline panel with power and HR on top of each other, a zone bar and three text rows. After: one chart per page (power · heart rate · cadence · target vs actual) using the centre of the screen, axis values at the corners, the target band shaded with its mid-line, the current value and its 30 s trend in the header, 'now' at the right edge, page dots for the swipe.

Navigation

Before: a fixed 6 m/px dial where the breadcrumb was a squiggle clamped to the rim and the route ahead was absent. After: auto-scaled map dial (2–25 m/px in 1-2-5 steps, scale bar), position at 60 % down in heading-up so the road ahead has room, the route AHEAD in accent, the trail in grey, the next turn marked, N on the rim, and one state line per situation. Points outside the dial become gaps, never rim artefacts. All coordinates in the gallery are simulated (labelled SIM).

Orbit

Before: four competing labels around the clock ('on target · 72 % · 4 312', sleep, source, next). After: two arcs with tracks, a 54 px clock, date + battery on one line, the next activity in 14 px, and ONE legend line that says what the arcs mean (LOAD 65 % · RECOVERY GOOD) with colour dots.

Chrono

Before: no hands in the render, dials in the hand sweep. After: real hour/minute hands (32 / 46 px) inside a 54 px hub, the three dials pushed out to radius 78 where the hands can never cover them, tick ring with hour marks, digital time + date between the lower dials.

Pulse

Before: a dense two-line sentence truncated with an ellipsis. After: one insight of ≤ 22 characters (SLEEP SHORT · GO EASY) with its grade dot, a 58 px clock, and a visible More.

Strength

Before: 'Set 2/3 · 8 reps · 60 kg' and a 60 px count with Log set / Undo. After: the exercise name (two lines, More when longer), SET 2/3 · 8 REPS · 60 kg · A1 (superset slot), REPS · AUTO 0.9 so the confidence is never hidden, the count dominant, Log set + Correct; Pause / End / Correct reps live on the workout controls overlay and Key 1.

Recovery

Before: a status word ('Good') led. After: the ANSWER leads — one insight (HIGH LOAD · EASY DAY) with its grade dot — and the measurements that produced it sit under it with their source and time.

3 · Cycling screens in the six appearances

Theme accents change; the grading colours (red / yellow / light blue / green) do not. Zone ladder = lead metric's zones (7 power zones from FTP, or the athlete's HR zones); target band = the step's band; white dot = current value; bottom arc = share of the interval completed.

4 · GPS: tracking, route and disconnected states

Three meaningful states, each with its own words (§6: never a combined warning). The watch has no GPS of its own; this is a mirror of the iPhone's fixes over Bluetooth, and no map tiles are claimed. Coordinates: SIM (synthetic 1.5 km path).

5 · Performance graphs

One primary chart per page; swipe changes the metric (page dots). Window = last 1 min here (5 min and whole ride use 1 s → 10 s buckets). Gaps in the data break the trace; no sample is ever interpolated. Axis: rounded min / max from the data and the target; target band shaded with its mid-line; the dot is the latest sample.

6 · Strength and rep counting

The detected count is the dominant element and its label says HOW it was counted (AUTO with confidence, or MANUAL). The athlete confirms or corrects every set; no accuracy is claimed until real exercises have been tested.

7 · Recovery and coaching

8 · Two-press physical keys

First press shows the key's name and action on a chip (15 ms haptic); the second press of the same key within 2.5 s executes it (action haptic). A different key replaces the preview; it expires on its own; a key press while the screen is off only wakes it. End = long Key 1 preview → long Key 1 confirm → the UI's own confirmation. Touchscreen Pause stays one tap on every live view; Reset/Boot recovery untouched. Tested: preview/confirm/expiry/replacement, accidental double press (80 ms apart → one event), presses while the phone is disconnected.

10 · Realistic missing-data states

Nothing is drawn without a live or valid source. A stale reading is never promoted to the lead metric; a missing sensor leaves no tile; unknown FTP removes the zones and the grade; a lost phone stops GPS points and marks sensor readings stale.

14 · Major design decisions

  • Single Pause pill instead of two buttons. Pause is the only control that must be instant; it stays one tap, 84 × 44 pt. Lap and End move to a controls overlay (tap the status line / swipe up) and to the keys with the two-press preview, freeing 50 px of the circle for the number and the gauge. Paused screens still show Resume + End on the touchscreen.
  • Commands are computed, not authored. `ride::command()` derives HOLD / EASE OFF / PUSH TO / CADENCE UP / HR ABOVE TARGET / INTERVAL mm:ss from the displayed target, so a contradiction like 214 W + 'reduce power' is impossible; the phone's long message is kept for the detail view behind the overlay.
  • 270° gauge with the bottom open. The opening carries the interval arc and leaves the control row free; the ring's inner edge (108 px) is clear of the status line (y ≥ 32).
  • One chart per page. A 240 px circle cannot show four graphs legibly; a swipe is cheaper than squinting.
  • Heading-up with the position low. The rider needs the road ahead; north-up is an option for people who think in maps. Scale is automatic (1-2-5 steps) and always shown.
  • Distinct status words. PHONE LOST · NO GPS · GPS STALE n s · OFF ROUTE · no route — never merged.
  • Chrono dials outside the hand sweep. Hands 32 / 46 px in a 54 px hub; dials at radius 78.
  • Insights, not sentences, on faces. ≤ 22 characters; the sentence lives behind More.
  • Confidence is part of the label. REPS · AUTO 0.9 vs REPS · MANUAL — the count is dominant, its provenance visible.

15 · Outstanding limitations

  • Host render only: fonts are a measured substitute for the LVGL fonts; sizes and fits must be re-checked on the device with the real font metrics.
  • Nothing has been flashed; buttons, haptics, touch, BLE and battery figures are untested on hardware (needs the factory backup and the owner's approval).
  • All GPS data in this gallery is simulated (SIM path near 25.197 N 55.274 E); real fixes arrive over BLE in W2.
  • Rep-count accuracy is unknown until the W4 dataset; the UI deliberately shows the confidence and asks for confirmation.
  • Cadence page and the 'target vs actual' page share the power series in these fixtures; cadence targets come from the plan when available.
  • Chrono's hands are drawn by the host renderer as 2-point polylines; the device draws anti-aliased hands with LVGL.
  • The controls overlay's reason text is truncated with More on the host; the device scrolls it.
