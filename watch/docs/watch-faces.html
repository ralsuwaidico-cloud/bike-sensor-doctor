Cyclift Watch — three face concepts × six appearances

Design concepts for the LILYGO T-Watch 2021 (round, 240 × 240). Each face is drawn in the three theme families (Cyclift Midnight Blue / Arctic · Sport Graphite / Steel · Bloom Plum / Rose). The four performance-grading colours are the same in every tile by construction — they are not theme tokens. Values shown are placeholders in the layout a real watch would use; every live value on the watch carries its source and age.

Concept renderings (SVG), not firmware output. Fonts and exact pixel metrics will be set in W1 on the device; LVGL draws arcs, ticks and text the same way.

Orbit

Futuristic circular time with a training-load arc (accent) and a recovery arc (graded). Complications at the cardinal points; the centre is the clock, or the live metric during an activity.

Chrono

Premium analog/digital hybrid: a tick ring, three small dials (next session, weekly load, recovery) and the digital time under the hands.

Pulse

Minimal digital face with one contextual coaching line (graded dot + text) and a thin status bar. The quietest face; the coach line changes with the time of day.

Adaptive hierarchy (same face, different emphasis)

Morning: sleep and today’s session are the large complications. During an activity: the face is replaced by the live view (Cockpit / Exercise). After an activity (2 h): recovery and the results card lead. Evening: tomorrow’s plan and bedtime. The layout never changes — only which complication is large and which coaching line is shown.

Cycling instrument — Cockpit · Performance · Navigation (redesign, 8 Nov)

Host render of the redesigned live cycling views (not hardware-tested; nothing flashed). Every arc has a defined meaning and source; nothing is drawn without a live source; missing data is an honest gap, never an invented sample; the GPS view is a mirror of the iPhone's GPS and never claims the watch has its own. All 744 scenes (31 screens × 6 appearances × 4 text variants) pass the automated round-display checks.

Cockpit — all sensors, FTP known, interval 2

Performance — 1-min power / HR with gaps, time in zone, rolling averages

Navigation — heading-up breadcrumb from the phone's GPS, age, off-route warning
