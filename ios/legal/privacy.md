# Athletics — Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026. Athletics is a cycling, strength and hybrid-training app for iPhone and Apple Watch, made by its developer in the United Arab Emirates (the "developer"). Contact: ralsuwaidico@gmail.com.

## The short version

Your training data belongs to you. Without an account, nothing leaves your phone. With an account, your data is stored in your own account at our hosting provider so it can follow you to another device, and you can export or delete all of it at any time from inside the app.

## What the app stores

Profile you enter: name, birth year, weight, height, gender if you choose to give it, units, training goals, experience, weekly availability, FTP and heart-rate values. Training data you create or record: rides (time, distance, speed, GPS route, power, cadence, heart rate when a sensor provides it), strength sessions (exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, effort, notes), workout templates, your weekly plan and coach decisions. Device data: the identifier of your BikeComputer cycling device and its settings when you pair one.

## Location

While a ride is recording, the app uses your location to measure speed and distance and to draw the route, including when the screen is locked. Location is never used when no ride is running, is never shared with anyone, and stays part of your ride data under your control.

## Bluetooth

Bluetooth is used only to communicate with your BikeComputer device and compatible sensors. No Bluetooth scanning happens for any other purpose.

## Apple Health

If you allow it, the app reads workouts and heart rate from Apple Health to show them in your plan and history, and writes rides and sessions you choose to save. Health data is never sent to our servers unless it is part of a workout you record and sync with an account; Apple Health data is never used for advertising or shared with third parties, in line with Apple's HealthKit rules.

## Accounts and sync

Accounts are optional. When you create one (Sign in with Apple, Google or e-mail), authentication is handled by Supabase (hosted in Frankfurt, Germany). We receive your e-mail address (or Apple's private relay address) and a user identifier. Your training data is then stored in your account's rows, protected so only you can read them, and synchronised between your devices. Passwords are never seen or stored by the app.

## What we do not do

No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no analytics SDKs, no selling or sharing of personal data. The app makes network connections only to the account service when you are signed in, and to Apple for Sign in with Apple.

## Your rights

Export: Profile › Account › Export my data gives you a complete JSON file. Delete: Profile › Account › Delete account and all data removes your account and every record from our servers and from the phone, immediately and permanently. You can also simply delete the app; local data is removed with it. For any request, e-mail the address above.

## Children

Athletics is not directed at children under 16 and does not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version ships with the app update and the date above changes.
