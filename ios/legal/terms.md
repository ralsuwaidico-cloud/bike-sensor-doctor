# Cyclift — Terms of use

Last updated: 26 October 2026.

## Using the app

Cyclift helps you plan and record cycling and strength training. It is free and provided as is; use it at your own risk and within your abilities.

## Training guidance is not medical advice

Training plans, coach suggestions, zones and estimates (for example FTP and maximum heart rate) are rule-based calculations from the data you enter and record. They are general fitness guidance, not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and Cyclift is not a medical device. Consult a doctor or qualified professional before starting or changing a training programme, especially if you have a medical condition, an injury or pain. Stop exercising and seek help if you feel unwell.

## Measurements

Values from sensors and other sources (power meters, heart-rate sensors, speed and cadence sensors, Apple Watch, GPS) are shown as received and may be inaccurate; power readings are labelled unverified until you validate them. The app never invents measurements.

## Hardware is optional

Cyclift works with the iPhone alone. Standard Bluetooth sensors, an Apple Watch and the BikeComputer handlebar device are optional; no feature of the app requires buying hardware. Third-party sensors and devices, including their batteries and charging, are outside the app's control. Ride safely and obey traffic rules; do not handle the phone while riding.

## Your data

Your data stays on your iPhone and is yours; see the privacy policy. You can export it or delete it at any time in Profile › Your data.
