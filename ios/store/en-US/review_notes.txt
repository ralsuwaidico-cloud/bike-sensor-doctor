No account is needed to review Cyclift: on the first screen, under "Continue as guest", enter any first name and tap Continue (onboarding steps after that can be skipped). Every feature (planning, ride recording with phone GPS, strength logging, routines, coach suggestions, history) works in guest mode and offline, without any sensor or accessory. Optional accounts (Sign in with Apple, Google, e-mail) are for syncing across devices; e-mail accounts require confirming the address, so a demo account is not provided.

Sensors and accessories (all optional, none required for review): standard Bluetooth fitness sensors (heart rate, power, cadence/speed), Apple Watch (heart rate during a workout), Apple Health, and the BikeComputer, a separate handlebar cycling computer the rider may own. Without any of them the app records rides with the phone alone and shows "No power source" / "No heart-rate source" instead of values.

Background modes:
- bluetooth-central: keeps the connection to the rider's paired Bluetooth sensors and the optional BikeComputer device during a ride, so power, heart rate, cadence and recording continue with the phone in a pocket.
- location: records speed, distance and the route while a ride is in progress, including when the screen is locked. Location is not used when no ride is running.

HealthKit: with the rider's permission, the app reads heart rate during workouts and past cycling/strength workouts to show them in the plan and history, and saves finished sessions to Apple Health when the rider chooses. Health data is not used for advertising and is not shared.

Coaching is rule-based (no AI model); suggestions are labelled as estimates and not medical advice.

Account deletion: Profile > Account > Delete account and all data.
