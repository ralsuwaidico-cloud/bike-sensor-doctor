No account is needed and there is no sign-in in this version. Everything works on first launch: enter any first name and tap Continue (the remaining onboarding steps can be skipped). A demo account is not required.

BikeComputer: an optional Bluetooth accessory (a custom ESP32-based handlebar bike computer) that the rider may own. It is not needed to review the app; every feature is reachable without it, and screens that use it show "not connected" instead of values.

Phone-only ride: Ride tab > Start. The ride is recorded with the iPhone's GPS (speed, distance, route) — walk outside or simulate a location in Xcode. Standard Bluetooth heart-rate, power and speed/cadence sensors are optional. The Apple Watch app is a companion that provides live heart rate during a workout; it is optional too.

Background modes:
- bluetooth-central: keeps the link to the rider's Bluetooth sensors and the optional BikeComputer accessory during a recorded ride while the screen is locked.
- location: records speed, distance and the route during a ride while the screen is locked. Location is never used when no ride is running.

HealthKit: only with the rider's permission, the app reads workouts and heart rate (to show past cycling/strength workouts and heart rate in the plan and history) and writes completed workouts to Apple Health. No data leaves the device.

All data is stored on the iPhone. Profile lets the rider export all data or delete all data on this phone.

No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no third-party SDKs. The coach is rule-based; its suggestions are labelled as training suggestions, not medical advice.
