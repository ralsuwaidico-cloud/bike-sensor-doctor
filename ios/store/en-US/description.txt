Cyclift is a training app for people who ride and lift. It puts your cycling and your strength work into one weekly plan built around your days, records what you actually do, and suggests the next step from your own training.

A WEEKLY PLAN AROUND YOUR DAYS
• Choose Cycling, Strength or Hybrid Athlete mode.
• Tell the app which days you ride, lift, do both or rest, and how much time you have.
• Cycling hours and lifting hours have separate budgets; the plan never schedules more than you set.
• Move, edit, skip or replace any session. Sessions you change by hand are kept when the week is rebuilt.

STRENGTH ROUTINES AND LOGGING
• Quick log (Full Body, Upper, Lower, Push, Pull, Custom) with duration and effort.
• Detailed logging with exercises, sets, reps, weight or bodyweight, supersets, RPE and notes.
• Build your own routines from the exercise library, mark the equipment you own, and assign routines to your training days.
• See your previous weights and reps while you train; pause and resume a session.

RECORD RIDES WITH WHAT YOU HAVE
• iPhone-only rides with GPS speed, distance and a route map — no extra hardware needed.
• Works with standard Bluetooth heart-rate monitors, power meters, and speed and cadence sensors.
• Live heart rate from Apple Watch during the ride.
• Five ride layouts: coach view, large gauges, classic metrics, rings and a live graph. Every live value shows which source it comes from, and missing sensors are simply shown as missing.
• Optional: pair the BikeComputer, a handlebar accessory with its own display. It is not needed to use the app.

A RULES-BASED COACH
• The coach looks at the sessions you completed — never planned ones — and makes small, explained changes to your next sessions, such as adding a repetition when your last sessions support it.
• During a ride it adapts its cues to the sensors you actually have, and every cue has a "Why?".
• After a session, a short check-in (how it felt, and any soreness, discomfort or pain) lets the plan adjust your next sessions.
• Unverified power and a provisional FTP are labelled as such, and the coach says so when there is not enough history yet.
• Small changes are applied automatically and can be undone; larger ones need your approval. Fixed days and your own edits are never changed without your approval, except that no training is planned while the health check says to see a doctor or that you are ill.
• Before it plans hard training, Cyclift asks a short health check based on the PAR-Q+ questionnaire. Cyclift is a training app, not a medical service.

HISTORY AND PROGRESS
• Timeline of rides and strength sessions with duration, distance, heart rate, power, zones and completion status.
• Weekly review of planned versus completed training.
• With your permission, import cycling and strength workouts from Apple Health without duplicates, and save completed sessions to Apple Health.

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR IPHONE
• No account and no sign-in: enter a first name and start.
• Your training data is stored on your iPhone and works offline.
• Export all your data, or delete all data on this phone, from inside the app.
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

Cyclift gives training suggestions based on the data you record. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.
