# Cyclift — name availability and trademark check (26 Oct 2026)

**This is not legal advice.** It is a quick desk search by the development assistant: web search, public trademark
mirrors and App Store Connect's own name check. Before the final App Store submission (and before spending on
branding), the owner should commission a professional trademark clearance search (UAE Ministry of Economy, plus US /
EU / WIPO Madrid for the markets where the app will be sold) and decide whether to file a trademark for "Cyclift"
(Nice classes 9 — downloadable software, and 41/44 — fitness training information/services).

## 5. Assessment (for the owner's decision, not a legal opinion)
- App Store: the name is available and already set on the draft listing.
- Trademark: the only registration found for the exact word is dead and for unrelated goods. The nearest live risk is
  the fitness-studio use of "CycLift" (same idea, possibly unregistered) and the "CYC" studio marks. Different goods
  (bike racks, motorcycle lifts) are a lower risk.
- Recommended before submission: professional clearance search (UAE + US + EU/WIPO), check the two domains, and
  optionally file "Cyclift" in the UAE (and Madrid) for class 9 and 41.
