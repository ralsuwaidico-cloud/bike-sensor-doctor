BikeComputer iPhone app 0.5.0 (TestFlight build 27) — real-device checklist
===========================================================================
Record PASS / FAIL / NOT TESTED per step. Everything is simulator-tested only until you confirm it.
Board: firmware 0.5.0 (phone-link unchanged). Never USB and battery connected at the same time (HW-1).
Build-24 steps still apply; this list covers what is new in build 27.

iPhone — Profile and Plan
 1  Update to 0.5.0 (27). Profile opens as a menu of six categories; your name, FTP, weight, birth year, zones and
    rides from build 24 are still there (open Rider information, Heart rate, Power & cycling to confirm).
 2  Profile › Training profile › "Training days and durations": set Mon rest, Tue cycle 45 min, Wed lift 30 min,
    Thu cycle 60 min, Fri rest, Sat cycle 1 h 30 min, Sun other 45 min → Save. Plan tab: Tue/Thu/Sat are rides at those
    durations, Wed is Strength 30 min, Sun is Other 45 min, Mon and Fri are Rest. Durations read "1 h 30 min", never
    decimals.
 3  Plan: tap Saturday → Edit → 2 h → Save. The card shows "edited". Tap "Rebuild this week from my days" → the app asks
    before replacing; choose "Keep my edits" → Saturday stays 2 h.
 4  Plan: set the weekly limit (Training profile) to 3 h while 4 h+ of riding is planned → the summary shows the
    overage and concrete suggestions; no session disappears by itself.
 5  Plan: the History section lists your past rides newest first with duration, distance, HR, power, zones strip and
    "completed"; tapping one opens the ride report. A past planned session you did not ride shows "missed".

iPhone — Ride dashboard
 6  Ride tab, board OFF: dark dashboard, three gauges reading "—", pills NO SENSOR / NOT TURNING / NO HR SOURCE, coach
    box "READY", header tag POWER UNVERIFIED. Nothing scrolls; START · PHONE ONLY visible at the bottom (iPhone SE/mini:
    confirm nothing is cut off).
 7  Start a phone-only ride and walk 3 minutes: T counts, phase reads WARM-UP then MAIN after 5 min, distance and speed
    fill from GPS, the live graph draws lines only for signals that exist (none without watch/board), coach box says
    which sensors are missing. DETAILS › opens the map, sensors and classic tiles.
 8  Watch workout running: the heart-rate gauge shows bpm with "BPM · APPLE WATCH", the HR pill reads BELOW/ON/ABOVE
    TGT, the red HR line appears on the graph; stop the workout → gauge back to "—" within 6 s.
 9  Board ON (0.5.0) and paired, pedal: power gauge shows watts with the zone label (e.g. "Z2 · 40%"), cadence gauge
    rpm, pills follow the target (BELOW/ON/ABOVE), coasting shows COASTING / NOT TURNING, never a stale number.
10  During a board ride with HR + power live for 2+ minutes: coach cues appear (e.g. "Push a little — HR below target",
    "Pedal a little faster", "Keep it up"), the coach box colours and the EASE / ON TARGET / FASTER scale light up, cues
    are spaced (no nagging every second). Tap "Start cool-down": phase COOL-DOWN, no more push cues.
11  Pause / Lap / End from the dashboard work as in build 24 (one ride, right owner, results sheet, saved to History).

Apple Watch
12  Watch tiles still mirror the ride; Start/Pause/End from the wrist still route through the phone (unchanged).

After flashing firmware 0.6.0 (record NOT TESTED until then)
13  Profile › Power & cycling › wheel circumference, then board Connections › Speed shows the same value (entered on the
    board — not synced in this build).
14  Live data from a BLE cadence/speed sensor paired on the board shows the source "Cadence/speed sensor" on the phone.
