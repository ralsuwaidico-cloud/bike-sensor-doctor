## Shared-file edits (owner review)
- `Models.swift` (Store methods in the strength/plan sections only, no schema change, no new stored keys):
  `ensurePlan(now:)`, `assign` (done guard), `setWeekdayRoutine` (returns warning, keeps hand durations),
  `deleteTemplateEverywhere` (keeps id → removed state), `startStrengthSession` (done guard), `todayAction` (calendar
  tomorrow, no recovery day), `addStrength` (one log per planned workout, id dedupe).
- `ContentView.swift`: `@State day` + day-change check in the existing 1 s timer; `todayAction(now:)` call.

## Known limitations
- Owned weights (fixed dumbbell pairs, plates) are not modelled; the Why? says so and suggests a rep instead.
- A generated week uses the day's budget-fitted minutes; a routine longer than that shows "the routine takes about …".
- An unplanned "Start <routine>" adds a hand-made session for today even if the rider then closes without starting.
- Needs the real device: the whole routines journey on the glass, kg/lb typing with the decimal keyboard, the centre
  button at midnight with the phone locked, resume after the app was killed by iOS.
