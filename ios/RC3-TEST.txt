Cyclift RC3 test build (iPhone)

New in this build
- Onboarding: five screens (About you + training, Goals, Your week, Connections, Your plan). No health questionnaire.
- Home: week strip (bicycle = ride, dumbbell = strength, moon = rest); tap a day to open it.
- Plan: edits change the real workout and show a before/after preview; Plan changes keeps every version with Undo.
- Progress: consistency first; FTP with its source; strength top set (Est. 1RM optional); 4/8/12-week checkpoints.
- Ride: an Unknown FTP stays Unknown - targets use heart rate or effort (RPE), never invented watts.
- Strength: next-set card with one-tap logging and rest timer.
- Profile: Personal, Training, Devices & Connections, Settings (Health & safety has Pause training).

Needs real hardware (not verified): Apple Watch, BikeComputer, heart-rate strap, power meter, Apple Health import.
