BikeComputer iPhone app 0.4.0 (TestFlight build 24) — real-device checklist
===========================================================================
Record PASS / FAIL / NOT TESTED per step. Everything new in 0.4.0 is simulator-tested only until you confirm it.
Board: firmware 0.5.0 now. Steps marked [0.6.0] need firmware 0.6.0 on the board (kit prepared, not flashed) and will
show "needs firmware 0.6.0" until then — record them NOT TESTED for now. Never USB and battery connected at the same time.

iPhone
 1  Update to 0.4.0 (24). Profile, plan, rides and tile layout from build 21 are still there. Home opens with the coach
    line ("Today: …"), the workout table, "Why this workout?" and a Connections card.
 2  Tap "Why this workout?": plain-language text about your last ride / this week / goal; it says no sleep or HRV source
    is connected. Tap Alternative → list (easier, shorter, off the bike, skip); pick one → Home updates.
 3  Bottom bar: the middle round "Ride now" button is larger and raised; tapping it opens Ride and NOTHING starts
    recording. Start ride on the Ride screen still works as before.
 4  Connections (Home card or Profile › Devices › Connections): rows for BikeComputer, Bluetooth, GPS, Internet,
    Apple Watch, power meter, strap, speed/cadence, Apple Health, WHOOP, Garmin. Each row opens a page with status,
    steps, measurements and troubleshooting. WHOOP/Garmin/strap are gray (not red).
 5  Switch the phone's Bluetooth off → Bluetooth and BikeComputer rows show a red "!". Switch it on → both return to
    green/amber without touching the board (on 0.5.0 the board must be awake with its screen on).
 6  Connections › Apple Watch › toggle "Use the Apple Watch" OFF → row gray with "off (your choice)". ON again → status
    follows the watch (ready / workout running / HR live).
 7  Profile › Delete account and all data → onboarding. Stage A: "Continue as guest" with a first name only. Stage B:
    skip birth year, enter weight, choose sports and goal, skip heart rate, let FTP be estimated, choose devices
    (watch on, strap off), Apple Health "Not now", summary shows "not set" for skipped items → Start.
 8  Onboarding again (Delete all data) and this time tap "Connect Apple Health" → iOS permission sheet → allow
    workouts. The Health row in Connections turns green only after that; deny → stays red with "permission not
    granted" (because you asked for it) — Not now → gray.
 9  Plan: tap today → "Add another activity on this day" → the day cell shows the ride plus a "+1" badge, nothing
    overlaps; tap the cell → day list → tap an entry → its detail. Check in dark mode and with the largest text size
    (Settings › Accessibility › Larger Text) that no card overflows the screen.
10  Profile: enter birth year 1990 and max HR 186 → "Max heart rate entered by you"; zones update; "Profile version N"
    increases by 1 per change. Kill and relaunch → values kept.

Board (firmware 0.5.0 today)
11  Connections › BikeComputer: shows link, firmware 0.5.0, signal, battery estimate; "Device settings and Wi-Fi" is
    replaced by the note "…need firmware 0.6.0". Sync status page says "needs firmware 0.6.0 (board has 0.5.0)".
12  Board ride start/pause/end from the phone and from the board behave exactly as in build 21 (one ride, one owner).

After flashing firmware 0.6.0 (with approval)
13  [0.6.0] First connection: Home shows "Phone and BikeComputer profiles differ" if they do → Sync status lists the
    differences → "Use the phone's profile on the board" → state "in sync"; the board's Home greets you with the phone
    name and FTP. Change FTP on the phone → within ~5 s the board shows the new value; Sync status "in sync".
14  [0.6.0] Device settings: move Brightness → board brightness changes and the page says "synced"; set Screen timeout
    2 min; set Wake gesture Double tap → board Settings › Power shows the same.
15  [0.6.0] Wi-Fi setup: Scan → your networks with bars and lock; iPhone hotspot appears only with Maximize
    Compatibility on and the Personal Hotspot screen open; enter a wrong password → "Wrong password"; correct →
    "Connected"; Forget → gone from saved.
