BikeComputer iPhone app 0.8.0 (TestFlight build 40) — real-device checklist, app side of the combined procedure
=====================================================================================================================
Order: 1) install firmware 0.7.0 on the board (INSTALL-v0.7.0.bat, approval first; TEST-v0.7.0.txt has the board side),
2) update the iPhone app to 0.8.0 (40), 3) run this list. Record PASS / FAIL / NOT TESTED per step. Everything is
simulator-tested only until you confirm it here. Never USB and battery connected at the same time (HW-1).
Rollback: TestFlight › Previous builds (36); board: ROLLBACK-TO-v061.bat / RESTORE-BACKUP-BEFORE-v070.bat.
